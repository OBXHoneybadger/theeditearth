Private-label products · by The Edit

Rooted in Responsibility.

Grove represents The Edit's future private-label product line. After years of researching sustainable products and understanding best practices across industries, The Edit will be uniquely positioned to create its own products that meet the highest standards of environmental responsibility. Rather than criticizing poor practices, Grove demonstrates what sustainable products can look like when built from the ground up.

The company launches only after substantial growth and market experience. Initial products may focus on common household goods, personal care products, reusable items, and environmentally friendly alternatives to frequently purchased consumer products. Because of The Edit's extensive research database, Grove products are designed using knowledge acquired through years of evaluating other brands.

Grove strengthens The Edit by creating products that fully embody the organization's mission and standards. It also provides increased revenue potential while showcasing sustainability practices that other companies may seek to emulate. In many ways, Grove becomes a living example of everything The Edit promotes.
