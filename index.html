The idea

Founded on a simple idea.

The Edit was founded to change how we humans interact with and impact the environment. Our goal is to educate and empower people to live sustainably and make a real impact on the planet, especially in our coastal ecosystems, which suffer greatly from our actions.

Our mission

A more stable future for our health, our environment, and our planet.

And that starts with research.

How we verify

Every stage of a product's life.

From manufacturing and shipping to use and decomposition, we see whether a product meets The Edit's sustainability standards.

1

Research

We research every stage of a product's life: how it is made, how it is shipped, how it is used, and how it breaks down.

2

Report

Each product we review gets a report explaining why it did or did not pass, published in our product database.

PassFail
3

Verify

Products that pass earn The Edit seal of verification, so the better choice is easy to spot.

Every product report is written to be clear for all ages and reading levels, from elementary students to older adults. When sustainability is easy to understand, it becomes the easy choice too.
